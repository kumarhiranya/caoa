# P11d — CAD retrieval over ShapeNetV2 (in progress, 2026-11-03)

Framework completion, not a paper contribution (user, 2026-10-29); the alignment evaluations keep the GT CAD.
Design (user decisions 2026-11-03): learned joint scan–CAD embedding + re-ranking; the database is every ShapeNetV2
model, searched within the query's synset (default) or over all; query = leak-free completion + instance points.
Code: `caoa/retrieval/`, `scripts/build_shapenet_points.py`, `scripts/train_retrieval.py`, `scripts/rerank_retrieval.py`.
DGX job 134281 (dgx05), one A100.

## Database

52,472 of 52,491 ShapeNetV2 models (the 19 missing are cars without `model_normalized.obj`; no Scan2CAD CAD among them),
2,048 uniform surface points each in the CAD's own bounding-box frame: `shapenet_points_v1/bbox_2048.npz`, 1.21 GiB,
~11 min with 20 processes. All 3,049 Scan2CAD CADs are in it.

## Stage 1: embedding (`configs/retrieval/embed_comp_inst.yaml`)

Query and CAD encoders (OCAM trunk, emb 512 → 256-d), InfoNCE against the GT CAD with 4 same-synset + 64 random
negatives per step, batch 64, 10k steps (AdamW 1e-4, cosine), 0.9 s/step (2.5 h). Train on the 6,663 `relpose_cache`
train pairs, evaluate on the 1,864 val pairs. CD-L1 (×1000) between the top-1 and the GT CAD, both in their bbox frame;
"random" = a random CAD of the GT synset.

| Search | recall@1 / @10 / @50 | median GT rank | category acc. | CD-L1 top-1 vs GT (random) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| same synset, yaw ×8 | 8.7 / 34.7 / 61.6 | 24 | 100 | **54.5** (91.1) |
| all 52k, yaw ×8 | 6.4 / 30.0 / 52.5 | 41.5 | 83.1 | 61.4 (91.1) |
| same synset, no yaw averaging | 8.2 / – / – | | 100 | 54.8 |

Per class (same synset): recall@10 bathtub 82, other 65, bookshelf 48, cabinet 47, chair 29, table 25, sofa 22; chairs
and tables have the largest databases (6.8k / 8.4k). Over all synsets, chairs keep their category 94% of the time,
cabinets / bathtubs / "other" 47–60%.

## Stage 2: re-ranking with the Siamese OCAM (`scripts/rerank_retrieval.py`, k = 10)

Each of the top-10 candidates goes through the P10b Siamese OCAM (separate trunks) as its CAD input (8,192 points + SEM
`cad_bbox` embedding, sampled as trained); the candidate is placed in the predicted box and scored against the object.
1,741 unique candidate CADs; the whole run takes a few minutes.

| Selection | Exact hit | CD-L1 to GT CAD | Legacy metric (box) | Rotation / scale acc. | S2C criteria −center inst / class | plain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stage-1 top-1 | 8.7 | 54.5 | 80.58 | 92.2 / 85.0 | 34.7 / 24.8 | 37.5 |
| Re-rank: CD to completion | 5.7 | 54.3 | 82.24 | 92.9 / 86.5 | 35.0 / 36.2 | 37.5 |
| Re-rank: instance → CAD | 7.5 | 54.5 | 81.97 | 92.9 / 86.1 | 33.1 / 22.5 | 36.1 |
| **Re-rank: sum** | 7.0 | **53.4** | **82.35** | 92.8 / 86.5 | 34.3 / 35.9 | 36.8 |
| Oracle in top-10 (closest to the GT CAD) | 34.7 | 24.7 | 82.14 | 92.7 / 86.6 | 50.3 / 41.0 | 46.2 |
| GT CAD (same pipeline) | 100 | 0 | **83.69** | 93.0 / 88.0 | 83.4 / 78.0 | 63.7 |

- **Sanity:** the GT-CAD row reproduces P10b (83.80 legacy, 83.4 / 78.6 S2C criteria) through the new sampling and SEM path.
- **Pose:** with a retrieved CAD the box is 1.3–3.1 points worse than with the GT CAD on the legacy metric; re-ranking
  recovers half of the gap (80.6 → 82.4).
- **Shape:** the fit scores barely improve the retrieved shape (CD 54.5 → 53.4), while the top-10 holds much better
  candidates (oracle 24.7): the fit score picks CADs that fit the box, not the GT shape. Open.
- **S2C criteria collapse for any CAD other than the GT one (≤ 50% even for the oracle).** Not a bug: the CAD boxes
  match the annotations' `bbox` / `center` convention exactly (checked). Scan2CAD compares the CAD's alignment parameters
  (the scale vector relative to the CAD's own size, and the translation of its origin), so a CAD with other proportions
  or another origin in `model_normalized` fails even on a perfect box. Reported to the user (Scan2CAD issue, rule 3).
